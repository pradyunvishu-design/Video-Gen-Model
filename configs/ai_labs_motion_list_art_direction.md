# Motion Expert Checklist Repair — Art Direction

## Beat

- Message: evaluate an automated result through accuracy, repeatability, and editability.
- Proof object: a three-stage review ledger using only the narration’s checks.
- Takeaway: a useful workflow is reviewable, not merely impressive.

## Concepts considered

1. **Three equal cards:** one card per check. Rejected as a generic bento/grid treatment.
2. **Circular score dial:** checks orbit a central quality score. Rejected because the score would be invented and the motion decorative.
3. **Asymmetric review ledger:** sequence rail at left, ordered verification rows at right, with each check revealed on its spoken beat. Selected because it adds software-context density without pretending to be a real product interface.

## Frame contract

- First read: “Three practical checks.”
- Second read: the three ordered checks and their questions.
- Layout: headline above an asymmetric 320 px rail plus a flexible review ledger.
- Type: Inter with sentence-case check names; small uppercase is reserved for utility metadata.
- Palette: near-black green neutral with off-white, steel blue, moss, and clay state cues. No yellow, purple, glow, glass, or gradient.
- Motion: panel settles once, sequence rail draws, rows reveal in order. Locked camera and no continuous motion.
- Provenance: no source credit because this is an original explanatory framework rather than quoted evidence.
