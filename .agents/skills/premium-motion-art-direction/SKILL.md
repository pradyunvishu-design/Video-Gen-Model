# Premium Motion Art Direction

Treat premium as a measurable design outcome, not a prompt adjective. This skill supplies the creative director pass before Remotion or HyperFrames authoring.

## Workflow

1. Reduce the beat to one factual message, one proof object, and one viewer takeaway.
2. Produce three structurally different concepts. Vary the relationship, not merely colors or camera angle.
3. Select the concept with the best relevance, readability, evidence fit, and originality. Reject any concept that needs invented UI, fake screenshots, or generated documentary imagery.
4. Write a frame contract: dominant object, reading order, grid, type roles, palette roles, safe area, source label, and maximum three focal groups.
5. Give every animation one intent: `micro`, `enter`, `connect`, or `emphasize`. Keep the camera locked unless a small evidence push-in is required.
6. Render representative opening, settled, emphasis, and exit frames. Critique hierarchy, legibility, spacing, brand fidelity, evidence, repetition, and motion coherence. Permit one automatic repair pass; then require review.

Use `configs/visual_style_profiles/ai-news-editorial-v6.json` as the machine contract and `configs/premium-motion.visual-style.md` as the portable style brief.

Apply the ideation gate before authoring: compare three non-equivalent information relationships, record why one won, and name the real proof object that makes the scene specific. Apply the five-dimension critique after representative frames are rendered: philosophy, hierarchy, detail/execution, function/specificity, and innovation/restraint. Any dimension below 3 fails; philosophy below 4 requires another iteration.

## Boundaries

- Extract general grammar from references; never trace frames, copy proprietary artwork, or imitate one creator's distinctive composition.
- Keep real source material and official brand assets dominant. Do not use AI-generated stills as news evidence.
- Brand color belongs to the authentic mark, an active state, or one evidence cue—not the whole canvas.
- Do not use decorative yellow or purple in editorial chrome, captions, cards, source markers, glows, or motion accents. Those hues may appear only inside an unmodified, verified official logo or source asset. Use the active neutral slate, moss, or clay role instead.
- Render provenance as a quiet newsroom credit with a short rule and square geometry. Never use a colored status dot, rounded pill, glow, or brand-tinted badge for a source label.
- Avoid generic AI defaults: glass cards, equal card grids, neon cyan, purple-blue gradients, random glow, gradient type, ambient drift, decorative underlines, 3D rotations, and constant zoom.
- Never invent a mascot, anthropomorphize a company mark, center a logo as the entire idea, or use stacked all-caps as the default headline system.
- For source text, an underline must use an exact single-line DOM Range stored with the capture. If the words or range are unverified, omit the underline.
- Use cuts or short fades for news pacing. Movement must reveal meaning.
