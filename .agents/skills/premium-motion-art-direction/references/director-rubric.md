# Director Rubric

Score each category 0–20. A frame passes at 85/100 with no blocker.

| Category | Pass condition | Blocker |
|---|---|---|
| Editorial relevance | The visual relationship explains the exact narration beat. | Decorative scene or unsupported implication. |
| Hierarchy | One object reads first, then proof, then takeaway; no more than three focal groups. | Equal-weight card soup or unclear focal point. |
| Typography and layout | 1080p type is readable, lines are short, spacing follows tokens, all content stays title-safe. | Clipping, body type under 28 px, or accidental crowding. |
| Motion | Every move has semantic intent; reading order and settling are clear. | Shake, continuous zoom, bounce, spin, ambient drift, or transition spectacle. |
| Craft and identity | Real assets, correct marks, restrained local brand color, consistent icons, and no generic AI-design tells. | Fake UI, invented mascot, warped logo, random glow, or unverified source. |

## Concept selection

Generate three concepts with different information relationships: proof-dominant, relationship-dominant, split evidence, sequential ledger, or open editorial. Do not call recolors separate concepts.

Choose by this order:

1. Truth and provenance.
2. Immediate comprehension at normal playback.
3. Fit to narration and available evidence.
4. Difference from adjacent scenes.
5. Visual surprise that does not reduce clarity.

## Frame review

Review at full 1920×1080 and mobile-size reduction. Inspect 0%, 35%, 70%, and 100% of the scene. Confirm that the first frame establishes context, the settled frame is readable, the emphasis identifies the narrated fact, and the exit does not interrupt reading.
